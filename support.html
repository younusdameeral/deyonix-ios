Common questions

  • How do I order? Open a section (for example Restaurants), choose a place, add items and tap “Send order”. Pay cash on delivery.
  • How do I book a doctor or barber? Open the place, tap “Book an appointment”, pick a day and time.
  • How do I add my business? Sign up as a business owner, open “My business” and add your details. The admin approves it.
  • How do I report or block someone? Use the 🚩 Report or ⛔ Block buttons on an ad, business page or chat.
  • How do I delete my account? Account → Delete my account.
